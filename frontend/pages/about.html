How It Works

🧠 The AI Model

DeepShield uses a deep learning model (convolutional neural network) trained to distinguish between authentic faces and faces generated or manipulated by AI (deepfakes). The model was trained on datasets containing both real and synthetic face videos.


When you upload media, the system:

  1. Detects all faces using OpenCV Haar Cascades
  2. Extracts and normalizes the facial region
  3. Passes it through the neural network
  4. Returns a fake probability score between 0 (definitely real) and 1 (definitely fake)

🔥 Grad-CAM Heatmaps

Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping (Grad-CAM) visualizes which parts of the image the model focused on when making its decision. Brighter/red areas indicate regions that most influenced the output.


For deepfakes, the model often focuses on: blending boundaries around the face, unnatural eye/teeth regions, skin texture artifacts, and lighting inconsistencies.

📊 Confidence Scores & Labels

✅ REAL

Fake probability below 40%. The model is confident the image is authentic.

❌ FAKE

Fake probability above 60%. The model detected likely deepfake artifacts.

⚠️ UNCERTAIN

Fake probability between 40–60%. Too close to call — manual review recommended.

👤 NO FACE

No human face was detected. The model cannot make a verdict without a face.

🎥 Video Analysis

For videos, DeepShield samples one frame approximately every 10 frames (up to 100 frames total, within the first 60 seconds). Each frame is analyzed independently. The final verdict is based on the mean fake probability across all face-containing frames, and the per-frame results are shown in a timeline chart.

⚠️ Important Limitations

No AI detector is 100% accurate.

DeepShield results are probabilistic. False positives and false negatives occur.

Not legal evidence.

Results must never be the sole basis for legal, criminal, or consequential decisions. Always consult domain experts.

Low quality images reduce accuracy.

Blurry, dark, very small, or heavily compressed images are harder to analyze reliably.

Model may not catch all deepfake methods.

New deepfake techniques emerge regularly. The model performs best on manipulation types it was trained on.

🔒 Privacy

DeepShield runs entirely on your local machine. Your uploaded images and videos are stored locally in the uploads/ directory and are never sent to any external server. Webcam frames are analyzed in real time and are never saved to history.


Files are automatically deleted after a configurable number of days (set in AUTO_DELETE_UPLOADS_DAYS in your .env).
